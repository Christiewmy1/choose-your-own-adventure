# HuskyAdvisor Website Copy Blocks

## Purpose

This document gives the frontend team ready-to-use copy for the first website version. It is meant to reduce filler text and keep the website aligned with the actual project scope.

## Landing Page

### Hero Title

HuskyAdvisor

### Hero Tagline

AI-powered academic and career guidance for UW Bothell students.

### Short Problem Statement

UW Bothell students often have to piece together advice from course pages, degree sheets, advising sites, and word of mouth. HuskyAdvisor brings that information together to help students choose majors, electives, and next steps with more personalized guidance.

### Example Questions

- I am a junior CSSE student. Which 400-level electives should I take if I want to work at Boeing?
- Should I choose CSSE, Applied Computing, or EE if I want to work in embedded systems?
- I am a Data Visualization student interested in healthcare analytics. Which classes should I focus on next?
- What should I focus on over the next few quarters to become internship-ready?

### Call to Action

Start Planning

## Profile Form

### Intro Text

Tell HuskyAdvisor a little about your academic background and goals so it can generate more relevant recommendations.

### Field Help Text

- **Major:** Choose the major you are in or the one you are considering.
- **Class Standing:** This helps the system keep recommendations realistic for your stage.
- **Completed Courses:** Add courses you already finished so they are not recommended again.
- **Career Goals:** Describe the kind of role, field, or skills you want to build toward.
- **Target Company or Field:** Optional, but helpful if you want more specific guidance.

## Recommendations Page

### Page Title

Your HuskyAdvisor Recommendations

### Explanation Text

These recommendations are based on your profile, completed coursework, and HuskyAdvisor's UW Bothell-focused planning data.

### Caution Text

These results are planning support, not official degree approval. Students should still confirm prerequisites and graduation requirements with official UW Bothell resources.

## Company Alignment Section

### Section Title

Local Company Alignment

### Section Description

This section connects your academic path to regional companies whose work overlaps with your interests. It is not a live job board, but it can help you understand which classes and skills fit likely career directions.

## Internship Prep Section

### Section Title

Internship Prep Plan

### Section Description

This plan turns your goals into next steps by highlighting useful courses, project ideas, and skill areas to focus on before internship season.

## Footer / About Note

HuskyAdvisor is a UW Bothell-focused project prototype built to help students explore majors, electives, and career-aligned preparation paths using structured local data.
